Your own AI key (optional)

Everyone without a key shares one free daily limit. Add your own free key and you get your own limit. It takes 2 minutes: open aistudio.google.com/apikey, sign in with Google, press “Create API key”, copy it and paste it here.

Home-screen widgets (Android app)

Show the next prayer and your upcoming tasks on your home screen.

  1. Tap Connect widget (once).
  2. Long-press an empty spot on your home screen → Widgets → Mustatar.
  3. Drag “prayer and tasks” or “next prayer” onto the screen.
